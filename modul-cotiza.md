# Modul-cotiza
## Main objective
Modul-cotiza is a platform designed to allow visitors of the modul.cl site 
to automatically and interactively explore the possibilities and 
benefits that come with a Modul modular home. In the site, they can select
their desired home arrangement, configure its details, and get an 
instant quote for an approxiamate price, so that they can then go on 
to speak with our executives and finalize the sale.

## Brand and design
Modul revolves around the concept of having a comfortable, cheap and
fastly built home anywhere in chile, so that you can enjoy the benefits 
of the wilderness of the mountains and the beach without dealing with 
complex construction costs with companies far away and who you don't 
have control over. With Modul, we build the entire home at our factory 
in Lampa and we ship it straight to the site, with the whole process 
finishing in close to a month.

UX wise, the site needs to be incredibly intuitive and step-by-step.
The finished product will feel more like walking through a tutorial than
using autoCAD to design a home as an architect.

## Important considerations
- It is really important to correctly convey to the user what they are doing.
Generic buttons with descriptive text don't cut it.
- We can't offer a price without making it clear that it is an approximation,
and that an official quote will be given when ready, by real people who undertand 
the user's needs.
- Actually designing the full plan, considering room positions and windows, is too
complicated for the scope of this program. We *need* to make assumptions in this area,
likely resorting to some automatic process to approximate where windows and
doors should be placed based on the module positions and their sizes.

## Minimum viable product
The MVP for this project needs to be able to, at least:
- Configure a home in a grid and visualize it.
- View a dynamic cost estimation in real time.

## Long term feature goals
- Agentic AI for house design based on the user's needs.
- Highly intuitive tutorial-like setup when first loading the page.
- Anti-bot protection.
- Professional-looking basic CAD design of the selected home configuration (Approximate).
- Exporting quote in PDF format.
- Support for / consideration in quote:
    - Choosing roller courtains
    - Asking for heating/AC
    - Different wall types
    - Water heating options
